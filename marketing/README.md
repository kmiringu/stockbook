# Selling the Hero welding rods

**Working prices.** These are an assumption based on the KES 1,150–1,200 per packet range, and Eliud still has to confirm them. They never go below KES 1,150 per packet.

To change a price, edit `docs/rods.html` and `make_flyer.py`, then reprint `rods-flyer.pdf`.

- **Public page:** https://kmiringu.github.io/stockbook/rods.html. Share it anywhere: it has call and WhatsApp buttons.
- **Flyer:** `rods-flyer.pdf` has two A5 flyers per A4 sheet. Cut along the dashed line.

## Who to go after, in order

1. **Hardware shops** in Gikambura, Kikuyu town, Kinoo, Zambezi, Thogoto and Ruku. One shop restocking 10–20 boxes is worth 40 walk-ins.
2. **Welding and fabrication workshops** (gates, grills, windows, trusses) in the same areas, and along Waiyaki Way and Southern Bypass.
3. **Contractors and building sites** with steel work going on.
4. **Fundi Kanga and Cashkad's own customers**, many of whom are fabricators already.

## Visiting a hardware shop (2 minutes)

> "Habari. We're Fundi Kanga from Gikambura. We've just brought in a big shipment of Hero welding rods, and we use them ourselves for our fabrication work. Do you stock rods? We sell a box at 4,700, and from 5 boxes it's 4,600 a box. For 20 boxes or more we can talk about a trade price. Here's our flyer with the number. You can scan it to see the prices any time."

Leave a flyer, and a packet if you can spare one as a sample. Write down the shop name and the owner's number. Call back after 3 days.
